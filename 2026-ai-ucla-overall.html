What it means

  • These 1,133 chose this option and no purpose, so the purpose chart leaves them out.
  • It is their own report about coursework. It does not record a course rule, a preference, or whether they had access.
  • It is not the same count as Never on the tool question. 1,330 of 6,703 marked Never on every tool they rated. Text Never is 25.4% of students who rated text tools.

How to use it

  • Say the base. A purpose percent is a share of the 5,340 who named a use. This 17.5% is a share of the 6,473 who answered the checklist.
  • Plan for both groups. Guidance written only for current users misses these students.
  • Read it beside the purpose list, where users most often name brainstorming, research, clarifying, summarizing, and study materials.
